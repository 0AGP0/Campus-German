## Course Overview

The Intensive German A1 Course is a comprehensive program designed for beginners in German learning, aiming to provide a solid language foundation in a short time. This course, offered by CampusGerman, is prepared in accordance with CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards and aims to develop students' basic communication skills in German quickly and effectively.

The focus in this program is not only grammar teaching; it is the balanced development of listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. Thanks to the intensive lesson pace, students reach a level where they can use German in daily life in a short time. The course offers a strong start for candidates planning education in Germany, wanting to come to Germany with a language course visa, or planning to learn German for academic and professional goals.

The CampusGerman Intensive German A1 Course stands apart from classical language courses with its structured curriculum, experienced teaching staff, and student-oriented approach. Classes are planned to encourage actively using German rather than learning it passively.

## Target Audience and Prerequisites

This course is suitable for students who are new to learning German or have very basic level knowledge. It is an ideal program especially for the following groups:

- Those who want to learn German systematically and quickly
- Those with education, life, or career plans in Germany
- Students planning to come to Germany with a language course visa
- Those who want to lay a solid foundation for their academic or professional German journey

No German prerequisites are required for course participation. Anyone who is literate and ready to study regularly can be included in the program. CampusGerman informs students before the course and ensures they start the learning process with correct expectations.

## Course Content and Learning Goals

The Intensive German A1 Course comprehensively addresses the basic building blocks of German. The course content consists of the following topics:

- Alphabet and correct pronunciation
- Frequently used words and expressions in daily life
- Simple sentence structures and basic grammar rules
- Introducing yourself, asking and answering questions
- Short text reading and simple writing exercises

The learning goal is for the student to be able to communicate at A1 level in daily life at the end of the course, understand and respond to simple dialogues, and gain an infrastructure to confidently continue German.

## Teaching Method and Classroom Environment

At CampusGerman, teaching is conducted with a communication-focused approach that encourages active participation. In classes:

- Live speaking practices
- Group and pair work
- Listening and role-playing activities
- Examples for daily life

are used. Classrooms offer a supportive environment where students can comfortably ask questions and not be afraid of making mistakes. Instructors aim for each student's active participation in class.

## Post-Course Progress

Students who complete the Intensive German A1 Course:

- Reach CEFR A1 level
- Can transition to German courses at A2 level
- Acquire the basic language necessary for official processes in Germany
- Become ready for more advanced education and exam preparation programs

CampusGerman also guides students after the course on the next level and education planning.
