## Course Overview

The German Course for Economics and Management Sciences is a program specially prepared for students who want to use German at academic and professional levels in business, economics, management, finance, and similar fields. This course offered by CampusGerman aims to provide students with the language they will need in both academic and business life by combining general German knowledge with sector-focused content.

This program provides an important advantage for students planning university education in Germany or aiming for a career in Germany-based companies. In classes, not only vocabulary but also topics such as academic texts, presentation language, report writing, and professional communication are addressed. Course content is structured in accordance with CEFR standards and deepened according to the student's current level.

The CampusGerman German Course for Economics and Management Sciences enables students to use German as an active working tool rather than abstract language knowledge thanks to its field-focused approach. In this respect, it differs from classic German courses.

## Target Audience and Prerequisites

This course is suitable for students and professionals who want to develop their German knowledge in the field of economics and management. It is an ideal program especially for the following groups:

- Students studying business, economics, management, or finance
- Candidates planning university or graduate studies in Germany
- Professionals who want to communicate in a German work environment
- Those who want to follow academic texts and courses in German

At least A2 or B1 level general German knowledge is recommended for course participation. This level is important for the student to benefit maximally from the course content. CampusGerman makes appropriate guidance by evaluating the student's level before the course.

## Course Content and Learning Goals

The course content is based on German terminology and academic language frequently used in the field of economics and management. Within the scope of the program:

- Economics and business terms
- Reading and analyzing academic texts
- Presentation and report language
- Meetings and business correspondence
- Interpreting graphs, tables, and data

topics are addressed. The learning goal is for the student to be able to use German fluently, correctly, and professionally in their own field. At the end of the course, students can follow academic courses more comfortably and express themselves clearly in professional environments.

## Teaching Method and Classroom Environment

This course at CampusGerman is conducted with a field-focused and applied teaching method. In classes:

- Real academic and sectoral texts
- Presentation and case studies
- Group discussions
- Applied speaking and writing exercises

are used. Classes are kept small and active participation of students is encouraged. Instructors are experienced in economics and intensive German and support students individually.

## Post-Course Progress

Students who complete the German Course for Economics and Management Sciences:

- Strengthen their intensive German skills
- More easily adapt to university courses in Germany
- Can communicate in German in the business world
- Get one step closer to their career and academic goals

CampusGerman also guides students on advanced programs, exam preparations, or career-focused education after the course.
