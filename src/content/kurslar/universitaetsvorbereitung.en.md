## Course Overview

The Campus German University Preparation Program is a comprehensive education and guidance program specially designed for students planning university education in Germany. This program not only raises your German language level but also provides you with skills that will enable you to fully manage the transition process to university in Germany.

The program prepares students according to a roadmap extending from language level to university application, Studienkolleg preparation, and direct undergraduate education. In this process conducted in cooperation with various universities in Germany, participants receive professional support on all steps such as official processes, application documents, and accommodation while raising their German language skills to academic level.

The Campus German University Preparation Program provides students with holistic preparation by bringing together German language education and university placement services. In this way, students confidently and consciously embark on their educational journey in Germany.

## Target Audience and Prerequisites

This program is for students who want to receive university education in Germany and addresses the following groups:

- Those with a high school diploma aiming for undergraduate education in Germany
- Those wanting to raise their written and oral German level to academic level
- Those preparing for Studienkolleg or planning to apply directly to university
- Students seeking professional guidance on official application processes, visas, and academic requirements in Germany

Within the scope of the program, a German education plan starting from A1 to C1 levels is generally applied. In addition to this learning process, students are expected to prepare their current educational documents, motivation letters, and meet the requirements of German universities. Campus German evaluates students' current German level and starts them at the most appropriate level.

## Course Content and Learning Goals

The University Preparation Program offers comprehensive academic and application-focused content beyond German language education:

- **German Language Education:** Reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills are developed at all levels from A1 to C1.
- **Intensive German:** Reaching the level necessary to follow university courses, reading academic texts, and writing reports.
- **Application Preparation:** Preparing CV, writing motivation letter, translating and completing documents into German.
- **University Placement Counseling:** Document control, university selections, Studienkolleg or direct application strategies.
- **Official Process Guidance:** Opening blocked account, determining visa type, application calendar, and official procedures.

Thanks to these comprehensive contents, students are prepared to successfully meet linguistic, academic, and bureaucratic requirements in their university education in Germany.

## Teaching Method and Classroom Environment

The Campus German University Preparation Program is conducted with a student-centered, interactive, and goal-oriented teaching method:

- **Interactive Language Education:** Group work, role-playing, and daily communication-focused activities.
- **Academic Preparation:** Reading, presentation, and writing workshops for university courses.
- **Counseling and Coaching:** Support for application documents, motivation letters, and portfolio development.
- **Small Class Size:** Opportunity for high interaction and personal feedback.

Students are supported with one-on-one counseling and project work in addition to classes. In this way, the German learning process is expanded to be aimed at academic and professional goals rather than being limited to language knowledge.

## Post-Course Progress

Students who successfully complete the University Preparation Program:

- Become ready to apply to universities in Germany.
- Can smoothly transition to Studienkolleg if necessary.
- Acquire the language and academic qualifications necessary for direct university education.
- Arrange the necessary documents for official processes such as visa process, blocked account, health insurance.
- Receive support on adaptation to university life, education planning, and career goals.

After the program, students are ready to continue their academic careers in Germany confidently and with a solid foundation. Campus German offers counseling support to students in academic and career planning even after graduation.
