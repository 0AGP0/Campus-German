Campus German is a language school. **We do not provide visa consultancy** and are not involved in the visa process. However, we prepare the necessary course documents and certificates for your course registration.

## What We Offer: Course Documents

As a language school, we provide you with the following documents:

### Course Registration Certificate (Anmelde-/Teilnahmebestätigung)

- Confirms your registration for a specific course level
- Includes course level, start date, and relevant course information
- Issued in German
- Prepared within 1 week after payment is received

### Visa Invitation Letter (Optional)

- Issued upon request by the student/agent and within the scope of the relevant product
- Additional fee: +50 €
- **Important:** This letter is not a "guarantee"; it is a supporting document for your course registration.

## What We Do Not Offer

Campus German does **not** offer the following services:

- ❌ Visa consultancy
- ❌ Visa file preparation
- ❌ Appointment/application process management
- ❌ Representation before official authorities
- ❌ Involvement in the visa process
- ❌ Visa guarantee

**Why?** Campus German is a language school. We provide language education and prepare course documents. Visa decisions are made by official authorities.

## Bremen Ausländerbehörde Notification

After the course registration certificate is issued, Campus German may inform the Bremen Foreigners' Office about your course registration when deemed necessary.

**Important to understand:**
- This is **not visa consultancy**
- This is **not visa tracking/representation service**
- It is only a **notification** about your course registration

This distinction is important for legal clarity and correct expectation management.

## Document Preparation: Only for Fully Paid Courses

Campus German prepares documents only for fully paid course levels:

- Documents are only prepared for fully paid courses
- "Pre-registration certificates" are not issued for other courses
- If you purchased multiple courses together, documents are only prepared for fully paid courses

## Document Preparation: Timeframe

- **Payment method:** IBAN (bank transfer)
- **Document preparation:** Within 1 week after payment is received
- **Document language:** German
- **Delivery:** By email with relevant documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Campus German provide visa consultancy?**  
No. Campus German is a language school. We provide language education and prepare course documents. We do not provide visa consultancy.

**Can Campus German help me with my visa application?**  
We provide you with the necessary course documents. The visa application itself is your responsibility.

**What is the difference between a course registration certificate and a visa invitation letter?**  
The course registration certificate confirms your course registration. The visa invitation letter (optional, +50€) is an additional supporting document for your visa application.

**Can Campus German guarantee that my visa will be approved?**  
No. Campus German does not provide visa guarantees. Visa decisions are made by official authorities.

## Transparency and Clear Conditions

We share all guarantee conditions openly and transparently. During the registration phase, the scope, conditions, and processes are explained in detail, so every student starts the process consciously and informed.

## Conclusion: Clear Boundaries, Clear Services

Campus German is a language school. We provide high-quality German education and prepare the necessary course documents for your course registration. We do not provide visa consultancy and are not involved in the visa process.

If you have questions about our courses or course documents, we are happy to help. For questions about the visa process, please contact the relevant authorities or a specialized visa consultant.
