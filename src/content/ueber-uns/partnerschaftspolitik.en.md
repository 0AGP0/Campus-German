This section clarifies Campus German's agency/partner business model and creates a sustainable, transparent operation standard for both sides.

## Our Partnership Model

Campus German sees agents not just as "sales channels," but as supported stakeholders in the process. Operational processes run according to standards, not according to persons.

### Basic Principles

- **Transparency:** Commission, discount, payment times are predefined
- **Standard:** All processes are standardized
- **Support:** Active support is provided for partner sales and operational processes
- **Reliability:** Document SLAs and process commitments are clearly defined

## Partner Communication Channel (Single Door)

Official communication channel for all partner/agency processes:

**📧 partner@campusgerman.com**

### Principle

Registration, payment, documents, course start, change requests, and reporting are managed through this channel. Thus, the process is traceable and record-based.

## Campus German Partner Support

Campus German provides active support to its partners in the following areas:

### 1. Regional Sales Manager Support

- **Objection management in the process of convincing students about Bremen**
- **Program selection and product clarity** (which course, which format, which dates)
- **Quick coordination on questions arising in the process**
- **Support line that prevents partners from "struggling alone with students"**

### 2. Marketing and Sales Materials (Sales Kit)

The following materials are offered to partners:

- ✅ **Brochures / promotional documents**
- ✅ **Price list**
- ✅ **Banners / images**
- ✅ **Video content**
- ✅ **In the future:** Sending printed materials (planned)

**Purpose:** For agents to make fast and consistent sales.

### 3. Monthly Reporting

Reporting standard for transparent execution of partner processes:

- **Monthly reporting** is conducted
- **Report content:** registrations, payment statuses, start dates, process updates

## Commission Policy

### Commission Rate

- **Commission rate: 20%**
- Commission is valid for **language courses**
- (Applied including intensive / online / weekly language courses)

### Commission Payment Time

- Commission is paid **after the total amount is received in the Campus German account**
- **Within 3 months at the latest** to the company IBAN provided by the partner

### Invoice Obligation

- **Partners/agents are obligated to submit a commission invoice for commission payment**

**Principle:** The payment process is recorded for accounting purposes; it is not proceeded with verbal confirmation.

## General Discount Policy (10%) and Transparency

There is a general discount/discount framework at Campus German:

- **General discount rate: 10%**

### Partners can:

- Either **pass this discount on to their students**
- Or **include it in their own margin**

### Transparency Note

- Since this discount is also stated on course pages, students may notice it
- Therefore, it is recommended that partner communication language be managed **transparently** rather than like "hidden discount"

## Document Process Commitment (Critical SLA for Partners)

The most critical point in partner sales is the document. Campus German gives a standard commitment here:

### Document Preparation Time

**Within 1 week after payment is received in the account:**

- ✅ **Registration certificate** (Anmelde-/Teilnahmebestätigung)
- ✅ **(Optional) Visa invitation letter** (+50€)

are prepared and delivered.

### Additional Information

When deemed necessary, the Bremen Foreigners' Office may be informed about the course registration. **(Not visa tracking.)**

## Partner Process Rules (Operational Clarity)

Basic rules for smooth operation of processes on the agency/partner side:

### 1. Final Registration Rule

- **"Final registration" is not accepted until student information and payment confirmation are completed**

### 2. Placement

- **Starting level is determined by placement test**

### 3. Document Preparation

- **Documents are only prepared for fully paid levels**

### 4. Refund/Voucher Rules

- **Refund/voucher rules are subject to standard procedures**
- **Different promises by partners are invalid**

## Partner Application Process

For agencies that want to become partners:

1. **Contact:** Application to partner@campusgerman.com
2. **Evaluation:** Agency profile and suitability evaluation
3. **Onboarding:** Sales kit, price list, and process training
4. **Active Partnership:** Start with sales and operational support

## Summary

Campus German partner model is based on **single-channel communication via partner@campusgerman.com, 20% commission, commission payment within 3 months (with invoice requirement), monthly reporting, and sales kit support**; the document process is conducted with **1-week SLA after payment** and all operational rules are transparently standardized.
