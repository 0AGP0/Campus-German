Campus German treats agencies as process partners — not just a sales channel. This page **does not publish commission or discount rates**; terms are shared after we review your application.

## What you can expect

- A single communication channel and traceable workflows
- Sales kit (price list, materials, content) after approval
- Support on product choice, dates, and documents
- Clear operating rules (enrolment, payment, paperwork)

## Apply

Send a short profile and your region — we will reply with next steps.
