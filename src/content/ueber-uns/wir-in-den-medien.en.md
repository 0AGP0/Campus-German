As CampusGerman, we take pride in sharing our successes in the field of education and our students' stories through the media. Being present in the media is an important tool both to show our education quality and to reach more students.

![We in the Media](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1504711434969-e33886168f5c?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Our Television Presence

On local and national television channels, news about our education programs, student success stories, and teaching methods are broadcast.

### Education Programs

On television channels, our expert opinions on German education and language learning are shared. These programs offer valuable information to students and parents.

### Success Stories

Our students' success stories are broadcast as special news on television channels. These stories inspire other students and show our education quality.

## Press Publications

In newspapers and magazines, news and articles about our education quality, student successes, and education trends are published.

### Education News

In local and national newspapers, news about our education programs, new courses, and student successes appear. These news items raise society's awareness of education.

### Expert Articles

In education magazines, our expert articles on topics such as German learning, language education methods, and career planning are published. These articles make valuable contributions to the education sector.

## Digital Media

On online platforms, our blog posts, educational content, and social media posts reach a wide audience.

### Blog Posts

On our website and other online platforms, content such as German learning tips, education guides, and student stories is shared. This content offers valuable resources to students.

![Digital Media](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1460925895917-afdab827c52f?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### Social Media

On social media platforms, daily educational content, student successes, and learning tips are shared. These posts enable us to interact with a wide community.

## Radio and Podcast

In radio programs and podcasts, our expert opinions on educational topics and student stories are shared.

### Radio Programs

On local radio stations, programs on topics such as German education, language learning strategies, and career planning are conducted. These programs offer practical information to listeners.

### Podcast Series

In our education-themed podcast series, interviews with students, instructors, and experts are shared. These podcasts inspire students and provide guidance on their educational journey.

## Awards and Recognition

Our education quality and successes are honored with awards and recognition from various organizations.

### Education Awards

Our successes in the education sector are appreciated with local and national education awards. These awards are an indicator of our education quality.

### Industry Recognition

Our leaders and instructors in the education sector are recognized and awarded by various organizations. This recognition reflects our expertise and success.

## Media Collaborations

Our strategic collaborations with media organizations enable our educational content to reach wider audiences.

### Content Partnerships

Together with our media partners, we produce educational content and student stories. These partnerships contribute to the spread of quality educational content.

### Sponsorship Programs

By sponsoring educational events and programs, we contribute to the education sector. These programs offer opportunities to students and increase education quality.

## Future Media Plans

We are continuously developing new media projects and working to make our educational content accessible to wider audiences.

### New Platforms

We have plans to be present on new media platforms and produce content in different formats. We are developing projects such as video content, interactive educational materials, and online seminars.
