Campus German is a language school. Our main service is German education, level management, conversation practice system, and course-related documentation. Services outside of this are not within Campus German's field of activity.

## Our Basic Position

Campus German's main service includes:

- **German education** (Bremen/Online/Hybrid)
- **Level management**
- **Conversation practice system**
- **Course-related documentation**

## We Do Not Provide Visa Consultancy and Visa Process Management

Campus German:

- ❌ **Does not provide visa consultancy**
- ❌ **Does not prepare visa files**
- ❌ **Does not manage appointment/application processes**
- ❌ **Does not represent students before official institutions**
- ❌ **Does not get involved in visa processes**

### Important Distinction

Campus German prepares the necessary course documents within the scope of course registration. When deemed necessary, the Bremen Foreigners' Office may be "informed about course registration"; this is not visa tracking or consultancy.

## We Do Not Provide Academic Consultancy and Guidance

Campus German:

- ❌ **Does not provide university/department selection consultancy**
- ❌ **Does not provide academic career planning service**
- ❌ **Does not manage application files (uni-assist, etc.)**
- ❌ **Does not track university processes on behalf of students**

### Note

The service offered under "University Pathway" is not general consultancy. Within this scope, only the defined product is offered (e.g., conditional admission support 500€) and the scope is not exceeded.

## We Do Not Give Guarantees

Campus German:

- ❌ **Does not give visa guarantee**
- ❌ **Does not give admission guarantee**
- ❌ **Does not give exam passing guarantee**
- ❌ **Does not commit that any official result will be "certain"**

### Why?

These results depend on the evaluation of official authorities and exam authorities. Campus German only provides educational services and commits to educational standards.

## For Our Partners

Campus German partners/agents:

- ❌ Cannot give visa or admission guarantee on behalf of Campus German
- ❌ Cannot make out-of-scope promises like "consultancy included"
- ❌ Cannot make promises that change or expand refund/voucher rules

Such statements create false expectations with students, lead to disagreements, and create risks in the partner relationship.

## What Do We Offer?

As Campus German, we offer the following services:

✅ **German Education**
- Intensive academic courses (A1-C1)
- Online courses
- Flexible weekly courses
- Exam preparation programs

✅ **Course Documents**
- Course registration certificate (Anmelde-/Teilnahmebestätigung)
- Optional visa invitation letter (+50€)

✅ **Educational Support**
- Placement test
- Speaking club (lifetime for students who purchased 3+ courses)
- Hybrid education model
- Cultural activities

✅ **Temporary Accommodation Support**
- Temporary accommodation options in Bremen
- Check-in support
